Drop a .glb, a .gltf, or its whole folder here — as many models as you like

Rename scenes, nodes, meshes, materials, animations & more inside .glb / .gltf files, and wire textures into material slots — entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Drop the folder to bring the .bin and textures along, so the preview shows the model exactly as it is. Drop another model any time to add it to the scene — or start from an empty one with + in the Scene tab.

    Click an animation to pick it, and its ▶ to play or pause it. Double-click one to rename it. Space plays and pauses from anywhere. Speed, Reverse and Loop apply to every open model. Playing is preview-only and never changes the file.
